# T-060 Phase 2 cleanup backlog

**Stage:** 5 Cleanup · **Depends on:** T-053 · **Type:** Backlog

## Goal

Collect phase 2 improvements in one place, to be split into their own tickets when picked up. Phase 2 cleans up content and makes things easier to find. It is not a redesign.

## Candidates

- Clinical and copy review of migrated pages; refresh outdated content.
- Review old syndicated news (for example 2009-era articles) to keep, update or retire, with redirects for any retired articles.
- Transcripts for videos that lack them (T-033 list).
- Better links between related content: service ↔ FAQ ↔ handout ↔ news.
- Banner dismissal and more placements (T-013).
- A site preview of drafts for the editor, if publish-then-check proves painful (PLAN.md defers this).
- Analytics review after 60 days: which CTAs and pages perform, and whether GA4/Matomo is worth it.
- Revisit sharing buttons only if the clinic wants them.
- Future scope that needs its own design (not phase 2 by default): a patient portal, online booking integration, and refill status.
