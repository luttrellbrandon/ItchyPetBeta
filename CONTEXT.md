# Veterinary Dermatology Center

The public website helps pet owners understand specialty care, prepare for a visit, find clinic information, and reach patient service workflows.

## Language

**Patient**:
The animal receiving veterinary care.
_Avoid_: customer (when referring to the animal)

**Client**:
The pet owner or representative seeking care for a patient.
_Avoid_: patient (when referring to the human)

**Appointment request**:
A client's expression of interest in arranging a visit. It is distinct from a confirmed appointment, which the clinic or its booking provider confirms.
_Avoid_: confirmed booking (for an unprocessed request)

**Prescription request**:
A client request for medication or allergy serum, including refill and new prescription cases. A submitted request is not an approval or fulfillment.
_Avoid_: medication order (when no fulfillment has occurred)

**Skin allergy questionnaire**:
The publicly linked questionnaire about a patient's symptoms, environment, and allergy treatment preferences. It is distinct from the new patient questionnaire described as being sent after booking.

**Promotion**:
Clinic-managed promotional content, separate from syndicated pet health news. Staff need to add and edit promotions.

**Content editor**:
The single clinic staff member who edits and publishes public website content. Publishing takes effect without a separate approval step.
_Avoid_: admin, webmaster

**CMS**:
The hosted tool the content editor uses to change pages, photos, banners, and other public content. It manages content, not client relationships.
_Avoid_: CRM, backend

**Banner**:
The default placement of a promotion: a card or strip on the homepage, optionally limited by start and expiry dates.
_Avoid_: popup (a popup is a different, non-default placement)

**Archive**:
The existing public news, FAQ, video, and supporting content planned for migration with searchable access and preserved routes or explicit redirects.
